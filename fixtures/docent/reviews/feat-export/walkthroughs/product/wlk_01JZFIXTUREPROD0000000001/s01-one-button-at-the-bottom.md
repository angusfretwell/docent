Before this branch you could make a palette you liked and then do nothing with it — the toolbar rolls new colors and the picker swaps presets, and that was the whole app. The dead end was the point of the design, and this is what fixes it {{capture:0}}.

Everything else on the card is a quiet pill on the card ground. The export button is deliberately the one thing that isn't, so the way out of the app reads at a glance without a label explaining it.
