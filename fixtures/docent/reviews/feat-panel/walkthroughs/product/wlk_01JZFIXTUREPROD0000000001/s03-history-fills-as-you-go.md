After three clicks the History panel holds three rows, newest at the top {{capture:0}}. A row is drawn as the palette compressed into a single strip — no names, no timestamps — because at this size the colors identify themselves faster than any label would.

The log is bounded at twelve entries. Older palettes fall off the end rather than growing the page without limit, which keeps the panel a browsable strip instead of an archive.
