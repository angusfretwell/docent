Restore puts a logged palette back on the main row {{capture:0}}. The recording restores the oldest entry, then the newest, then presses Reset to land back on the sand preset.

Restoring is deliberately not a new generation: the History panel does not grow while the recording plays. Only Generate appends, so browsing the log never rewrites it — and Export tracks each restore, so what you copy is always what you are looking at.
